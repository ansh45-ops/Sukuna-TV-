SUKUNA TV — RED & BLACK MOBILE UI
=================================
Extract the ZIP and open index.html in a modern browser.

Files:
- index.html: page structure
- style.css: responsive design and visual styling
- app.js: channel catalogue, filtering and playback behavior

The site loads a public playlist online. Channel availability depends on the broadcaster.
Only watch streams you are authorized to access. Some streams may be unavailable or region restricted.
